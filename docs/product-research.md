## Original programming choices

Balanced Foundations replaces the previous high-frequency plan with three full-body strength days per week, two easy movement days, and two rest days. Each strength session includes push, pull, lower body, and core work. Week 2 increases repetitions, week 3 increases sets, and week 4 returns to lower volume. Day 29 repeats day 1 without max testing; day 30 is review and recovery.

These exact sessions and targets are our implementation decisions, not a claim about any competitor's paid plan or a clinically personalized prescription. Calendar progression does not force harder exercise variations. Users select full push-ups or unassisted pull-ups only when comfortable; foundation mode overrides those choices. Session notes explain repeating earlier work and keeping clean reps in reserve.

Ten additional exercises have original descriptions and cues. New movements show photorealistic generated male exercise images alongside written technique cues. Existing visuals remain. Lower-body work, floor pressing alternatives, equipment-free filters, progression links, and a leg skill path expand the original library.

Equipment changes use explicit substitutions. Rows can replace unavailable assisted pull-ups as horizontal strength work, with that difference disclosed. When there is no usable pulling equipment, pulling is omitted visibly; shoulder mobility is not claimed to replace loaded pulling. The preview warns that the original workout focus may be incomplete.

## Records and limits

The rebuilt program starts separate completion storage. Original completion remains untouched and exports with the original plan. Old workout history remains labeled separately. Baseline/review comparison requires matching movement, unit, assistance, and set structure. Results are self-reported; this is not automatic form assessment or skill certification.

Profiles, favorites, and the latest 200 workouts remain device-local. The weekly attendance target includes recovery and does not reschedule the program. Focus affects dashboard guidance, while effort, equipment, and selected variations affect prescriptions. Shortening or substituting work labels the resulting saved session as adapted.

## Verification

Desktop/mobile tests check all 30 days across all 32 equipment combinations and both effort levels, full-body coverage and recovery spacing, week-four volume reduction, baseline parity, archive preservation, new exercise links, harder-variation selection, side-specific timing, and the existing guided-session flows. Build, lint, and visual browser checks accompany the tests.
